Remarque keeps your documents as ordinary UTF-8 Markdown files. The left pane shows the source; the right pane is an editable rendered document. Both panes share one document and one undo history.

Choose a template in the Start window, or use File → Open. Select a heading, word, table cell, equation, or diagram on the right to reveal its corresponding source. Selecting on the right keeps keyboard focus on the right.

The right toolbar has three tabs: **markdown**, **Mermaid**, and **LaTeX**. Use Command-Z to undo an edit from either pane. Save normally with Command-S. Local recovery drafts and version history provide additional protection.
