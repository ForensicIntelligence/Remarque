The right pane renders Markdown, tables, code, math, and diagrams locally. It is also an editor: typing and formatting update the left pane immediately.

Settings → Preview controls scroll synchronization and front matter visibility. Scroll synchronization follows document positions rather than matching text, so repeated paragraphs remain distinct. Selection-to-source navigation works independently of scrolling.

Invalid math or Mermaid source remains editable and shows an error instead of being removed.
