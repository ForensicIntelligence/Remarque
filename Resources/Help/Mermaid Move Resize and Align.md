Selected diagram cards expose Source, Visual Edit, Zoom, Layout, move up/down, Copy Source, SVG, and PNG. Layout accepts width, height, title, and left/center/right alignment.

Drag the lower-right handle to resize a card. Dimensions are saved when the drag ends. Move controls swap a diagram with its adjacent document block. Undo restores the previous position or size.

Zoom opens a larger view. Use the wheel to change zoom. Visual-editor node positions and document card placement are separate controls.
