Privacy & Local-First

Remarque does its work entirely on your Mac.

**No** telemetry, analytics, or usage tracking

**No** network calls — Mermaid renders locally, never from a CDN

**No** accounts, cloud sync, or document upload

Your documents remain ordinary Markdown files in locations you choose. Diagram layout is stored as a small, optional comment that other editors simply ignore. Mermaid rendering runs at the strict security level and never executes diagram scripts.

Sandboxed, hardened & offline

Remarque runs in the macOS **App Sandbox** with a hardened runtime and makes **no network calls whatsoever** — every embedded web view refuses any navigation that isn't a local file, and the bundled Mermaid and math engines render entirely on-device.

The sandbox also means opening a document grants Remarque access to **that file only**. When a document displays images stored next to it, Remarque asks you to approve the document's folder the moment it opens — a one-time grant, remembered across launches, that also covers exporting those images. Decline and a banner above the preview offers the grant again, on your terms. Remarque never reads anything you haven't explicitly opened or approved.

Controls, tool palettes, and diagram cards carry **VoiceOver** labels; canvas nodes are reachable as accessibility elements; and the editor and preview text scale to your preferred sizes. The interface is fully keyboard-navigable.

Syntax highlighting

Markdown structure is gently emphasized: headings stand out, markers are subdued, links are accented, and fenced `mermaid` blocks get their own diagram color. Highlighting is **incremental** — only the line you edit is re-styled — so it stays instant even in very large documents.
